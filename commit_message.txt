Fix: Reset density matrix and atomic coords cleanly during BEC calculation

The Born Effective Charge (BEC) calculation loop evaluates structures across atomic displacements but was suffering from the following:
1. "Charge density from K is strange: Infinity" due to invalid re-use of K/L matrices generated from unshifted structures.
2. Coordinates not correctly updated in the spatial partitioning arrays causing the static energy bug and 0 values for Z*.

Changes:
- Replaced `update_atom_coord` + `updateIndices3` sequence with the proper `update_pos_and_matrices(0)` + `update_H` wrappers.
- Explicitly disabled `flag_LmatrixReuse`, `flag_DM_converged` and `restart_DM` within `bec_run` loop.
- Implemented `flag_reset_dens_on_atom_move` manually during BEC displacements to force full exact SCF re-convergence per displacement.
